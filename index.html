Privacy Policy

eZee Sales Assistant – Mr. Octopus & BR

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Purpose

This assistant helps authorised business users retrieve and analyse sales reports for Mr. Octopus and Basking Robbins.

Information processed

When requested, the assistant may process sales records, item names, quantities, transaction dates, payment information, discounts, taxes and related business reporting data retrieved through the eZee Optimus API.

Use of information

Information is used to prepare sales summaries, compare outlet performance, rank items and support business decisions.

Security

Access to the reporting service is protected by authentication controls. API credentials should be kept confidential and must not be shared in conversations or published in this repository.

Third-party services

The service uses OpenAI's ChatGPT platform, Cloudflare infrastructure and the eZee Optimus API. Each provider's own privacy terms may apply to data it processes.

Retention and contact

Data handling and retention may depend on the configuration and policies of the services involved. For questions about this assistant or its use of business data, contact the business administrator for Mr. Octopus and Basking Robbins.
